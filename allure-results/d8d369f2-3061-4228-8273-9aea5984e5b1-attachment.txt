To apply for a personal loan, you typically need to follow these steps:

1. **Check Eligibility**: Review the eligibility criteria set by your bank, including income, credit score, and employment status.

2. **Gather Documents**: Prepare necessary documents such as identity proof, address proof, income proof, and bank statements.

3. **Visit the Bank or Apply Online**: You can either visit your bank's branch or apply through their official website or mobile app.

4. **Fill Out the Application**: Complete the personal loan application form with accurate information.

5. **Submit Documents**: Provide the required documents along with your application.

6. **Loan Processing**: The bank will review your application and documents, and may conduct a credit check.

7. **Approval and Disbursement**: If approved, you will receive a loan offer detailing the amount, interest rate, and tenure. Upon acceptance, the loan amount will be disbursed to your account.

For specific details and assistance, please contact your bank directly.
